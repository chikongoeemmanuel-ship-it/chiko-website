Engineered For Precision

Two Specialized Systems. One Unified Standard.

While sharing central financial ledgers and executive analytics, each ERP is customized from the ground up for the distinct demands of clinical healthcare and agricultural logistics.

Chiko Pharmacy ERP

Clinical Governance & Dispensary Intelligence

Built in strict conformity with national pharmacy boards, WHO Good Distribution Practice (GDP), and strict pharmacovigilance standards.

  • FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) Logistics
    Automated dispensing rules prioritize closest expiration batches to prevent expired stock and eliminate write-offs.
  • Controlled Narcotics (Schedule II-IV) Audits
    Dual-signature pharmacist sign-offs, doctor license verification, and real-time tamper-evident dispensing ledgers.
  • Direct Physician e-Prescription Exchange
    Encrypted FHIR/HL7 integration enabling hospital wards and outpatient clinics to transmit verifiable digital prescriptions.
  • Cold-Chain Biologicals Temperature Telemetry
    Continuous IoT monitoring of medical vaccine refrigerators with automated SMS and WhatsApp escalation alerts on thermal breach.

Chiko Agrovet ERP

Agronomy, Veterinary Care & Depot Logistics

Tailored for commercial agriculture, veterinary surgeon clinics, certified seed distributors, and regional farmer cooperatives.

  • Veterinary Clinical Drench & Herd Vaccine Tracking
    Dosage calculators based on herd weight classes, withdrawal period alerts for milk and meat safety compliance.
  • Agrochemical Hazard & Toxicity Classification
    Automatic safety data sheet (SDS) attachment, protective gear guidance, and compliance with pesticide control board rules.
  • Seasonal Planting & Fertilizer Demand Forecasting
    Predictive replenishment modules analyze historical rainfall windows and planting seasons to prevent stock-outs.
  • Farmer Cooperative Credit & Subsidy Ledgers
    Flexible revolving credit management, harvest check-off deduction schedules, and government voucher redemptions.
